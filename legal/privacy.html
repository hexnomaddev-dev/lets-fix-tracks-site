Privacy Policy — Let's Fix Tracks

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Let's Fix Tracks is developed and published by an independent developer. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to operate the game, provide its features and support purchases and advertising.

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Let's Fix Tracks.

1. Information We Handle

The game stores certain information locally on your device, including puzzle progress, completion statistics, game settings, daily hint usage and reminder preferences.

When you use advertising or purchase features, certain information may be processed outside your device:

  • Advertising: Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, consent choices and advertising interactions to provide and measure advertisements.
  • Purchases: Google Play processes your purchases. Purchase tokens and related verification information, including an installation identifier and device-integrity information, are sent to our purchase-verification service to verify entitlements and prevent fraudulent transactions.
  • Installation identity: The game creates an anonymous installation identity using a cryptographic key stored on your device. Its public-key identifier may be transmitted to our verification service.

We do not require you to create an account or provide an email address or phone number to play the game.

2. Third-Party Services

We use the following services:

  • Google Mobile Ads: To provide optional rewarded advertisements and other advertisements.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): To manage advertising consent where applicable.
  • Google Play Billing: To process in-app purchases.
  • Google Play Integrity: To help verify purchase requests and protect against fraudulent activity.
  • Our purchase-verification service: To validate purchases and maintain purchase entitlements.

These services may process information under their own privacy policies. Their applicable data practices and advertising partners should be reviewed through their respective privacy disclosures.

Google's privacy information is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Save and restore your game progress and settings on your device.
  • Provide and measure advertisements.
  • Process and verify purchases.
  • Maintain premium entitlements.
  • Protect the game and its purchase system against abuse and fraud.
  • Respond to privacy and support enquiries.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Storage, Sharing and Security

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and are not synchronised to cloud storage.

Information required for advertising and purchase verification may be transmitted to the relevant service providers.

App-owned network communications use HTTPS. We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by our services, but no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Your Choices

You can use the in-game player-data reset option to clear locally stored game progress, settings and related player data.

Uninstalling the game also removes its local application data and installation identity.

Information processed by Google or our purchase-verification service may be retained separately under their applicable retention practices and legal obligations.

For questions about information handled by our services, or to request assistance with a privacy matter, contact us using the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

Let's Fix Tracks does not require children to create accounts or provide contact information to play.

Advertising and data handling are subject to the applicable age, consent and advertising requirements. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for unrelated purposes.
